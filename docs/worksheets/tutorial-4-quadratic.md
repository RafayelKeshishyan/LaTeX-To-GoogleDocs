# Tutorial 4 — Quadratic Formula

**DigiMath Tutorial 4** | [Access Digi Math](https://accessuci.ics.uci.edu/digimath/)

## Learning objective

Combine fractions, square roots, exponents, and `\pm` in the quadratic formula.

## Instructions

1. Press **Alt+=** to insert an equation.
2. Type the LaTeX below.
3. Press **Enter** to hear natural speech.
4. Press **F2** to edit if needed.

---

## Practice problems

### Problem 1 — Quadratic formula

**LaTeX:** `x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}`

**Professional speech:** "x equals the fraction with numerator negative b plus or minus the square root of b squared minus 4 a c, and denominator 2 a"

---

### Problem 2 — Discriminant

**LaTeX:** `b^2 - 4ac`

**Professional speech:** "b squared minus 4 a c"

---

### Problem 3 — Simple quadratic

**LaTeX:** `x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0`

**Professional speech:** "x squared plus 5 x plus 6 equals 0"

---

### Problem 4 — Factored form

**LaTeX:** `(x+2)(x+3) = 0`

**Professional speech:** "open parenthesis x plus 2 close parenthesis open parenthesis x plus 3 close parenthesis equals 0"

---

## TVI notes

- This is the capstone tutorial — combines all prior skills.
- `\pm` is spoken as "plus or minus" in Professional mode.
- Long equations: encourage students to build in parts, using F2 to verify LaTeX.
- Use **Ctrl+Shift+L** to show LaTeX source when teaching sighted peers.
