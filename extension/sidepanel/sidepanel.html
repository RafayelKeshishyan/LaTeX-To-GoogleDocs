LaTeX Equation Editor

Linear mode — type LaTeX, then insert into your document

Keyboard workflow: put the cursor in your Google Doc where the equation should go, then press Alt+Enter or activate Insert at cursor. Alt+Shift+Enter starts a new line below the cursor and puts the equation there, which is how you add one between two existing lines. Enter alone adds a new line in the LaTeX box. Alt+D returns to the document (panel stays open). In the document, Up and Down move the cursor line by line and read out any equation on the line. F2 opens the equation on the cursor line for editing; press Alt+Enter to replace it in place, keeping its position. Ctrl+Shift+R re-reads the current equation. Ctrl+Shift+Delete removes the selected equation.
